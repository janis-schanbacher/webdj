Default demo tracks
===================

The MP3 files in this folder are included as the default decks in WebDJ.

Works
-----
- "Dreamland Overflow" (track 08, 74 BPM)
- "Kaleidoscope Tribe" (track 09, 74 BPM)

Album: Entangled Everything (2018)
Artist: Globular (Morison Bennett)
All music written and produced by Morison Bennett.
Design and artwork by Incedigris.
