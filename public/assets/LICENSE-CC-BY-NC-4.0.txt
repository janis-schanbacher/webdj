Default demo tracks
===================

The MP3 files in this folder are included as the default decks in WebDJ.

Works
-----
- "Patience" (track 01, 143 BPM)
- "Truth" (track 02, 143 BPM)

Album: Symphonies of Life (2018)
Artist: SubConsciousMind (R. Huber)
All songs written and produced by R. Huber @ SCM Soundlabs (2010-2018).
Mastered by Vince Le Barde. Cover design by R. Huber.
